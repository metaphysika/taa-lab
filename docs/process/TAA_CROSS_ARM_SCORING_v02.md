# Cross-arm scoring v0.2 — comparison review fixes

[DESCRIPTIVE] Ten unchanged development programs × eight arms × four scripted controllers =320 episodes.
[PROTOCOL] The committed matching protocol, keys, programs, controllers and ten frozen POEC contracts are unchanged.
[MEASUREMENT] One runner, actual-state audits and the common external duty oracle assess every arm.
[PRESERVATION] v0 and v0.1 reports, plans and evidence remain byte-identical.
[LIMIT] Offline scripted development evidence only; no empirical H3 validation or population inference.

## Limitations

Keyed metrics apply only while an arm’s context matches the ordinary-core trajectory. Early-diverging floor/static actions become unassessed; few keyed wrongful executions do not establish safety. Keyed counts must not be presented as a TAA advantage over the independently implemented conventional arm.

The TraceGrant reconstruction with fixed W0 contracts and documented adaptations covers only the ten declared programs. No post-outcome contracts are authored for frozen/regression/H1/Q14 tasks. It is not the authors’ evaluated configuration. Floor/static partial writes are retained in a separate fault supplement; receiptless closure/transfer operations are explicitly not scored by the unchanged receipt-based protocol operation metrics.

The supplementary measure is **committed acts disallowed by the reference policy**, **defined after v0 results; development only; to be pre-registered before Experiment A**. It counts PROHIBIT, REFUSE, HOLD and REFER in the arm’s actual pre-commit state. ALTERNATIVE is listed separately, not counted as one of those four and not declared safe. It supplements rather than replaces keyed metrics.

### What the table does not show

Attributed to Astra’s 2026-10-24 review; the list below is verbatim.

A skeptical reviewer would correctly say it does not establish:
- TAA superiority over strong conventional engineering—the reported protocol metrics match SQL.
- General superiority over published TraceGrant, rather than this reconstruction and contract set.
- Real-model alignment, prompt-injection resistance, or reliable natural-language act interpretation.
- Safety from zero keyed wrongful executions: divergent contexts become unassessed.
- Generalization from ten development programs, or statistical independence of the 320 episodes.
- Production reliability, distributed atomicity, or a causal benefit specifically attributable to Thomistic metaphysics.

Astra’s two additional oracle limitations:

- reference-derived scoring is a common normative evaluator, not independent ground truth about ethics;
- broader fault campaigns must account explicitly for receiptless closure and transfer operations.

The template-scope question is answered by Claude’s Q17 ruling; its implementation and unchanged-protocol verification are tracked separately from the retained v0.2 run.

Definitions remain those in the committed protocol and metric implementation. Integer metrics sum separately initialized episodes; the list-valued metric is retained for every episode below and in CSV/JSON. Audit checks are not independent trials. Actual receipts and world refunds/notices/cases are reported separately from keyed outcomes. No arm’s internal duty ledger is a scoring source.

C1 removes only the prohibition stage. C2 primary removes revision invalidation and execution-time reassessment; the diagnostic retains reassessment. Actor, exact arguments, single use, expiry and withdrawal remain token conditions in both. Floor/static duty tools are plain business operations. All four controllers are fixed scripts, with their unchanged program horizons.

The original keyed_disallowed_executions metric includes PROHIBIT, REFUSE, HOLD, REFER and ALTERNATIVE. The supplementary four-category count excludes ALTERNATIVE, which is listed separately. These are distinct existing definitions, not interchangeable totals.
