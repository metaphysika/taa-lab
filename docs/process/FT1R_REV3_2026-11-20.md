# FT1-R revision 3 (replaces revision 2), 2026-11-20

Status: **approved by Chris on 2026-11-20 ("One", choosing option 1, "Revision 3 complete"); to be frozen before any FT1 test part is pushed. Nothing built or run yet.** Revision 2 (`docs/process/FT1_ARM3_FOLLOWUP_DRAFT_2026-11-17.md`) is cancelled by Chris and kept unchanged as the record. This revision was written **after** seeing all 12 FT1 gate results (`LAB_NOTEBOOK.md`, 2026-11-20) and **before** any FT1 test-part result. It is the second amendment made after seeing gate results, and is disclosed as such.

## Why

The FT1 gate failures were diagnosed from the raw replies by Fable, with second opinions from Astra and Neon (all three in this thread, 2026-11-20; evidence `results/2026-11-20 v0.24.94 ft1-gate-* cb5b86577/` and `results/2026-11-17 v0.24.94 ft1-gate-* cb5b86577/`).
- **Arm 3** never practised ending its reason in the last position. It runs to the 1,024-token cap, or omits the closing `}` or `"args"`. Revision 2's reason-last layout targets this, and is kept.
- **Arm 2** breaks JSON when its restatement contains a quoted line:
  - it ends on the quoted pressure line with `\"}` instead of `\""}`;
  - it doubles a backslash after the quoted order line.

  Quote-free arm-2 replies were never malformed. Training cards contained no quotation marks.
- **Neon's check of the real FT1 arm-2 data** (`results/2026-11-13 v0.24.87 ft1-data-preparation 5b2b3638845c/kaggle-v3/arm-2.jsonl`): 0 of 2,000 restatements contain a quotation mark, and none of the 1,000 pressure records reaches the pressure line. The reason is that restatements are prefixes of the card, and the pressure line is appended after the three `Tools:` lines. Quoting the lines without moving the pressure line would therefore teach nothing.

## What changes from revision 2

Everything in revision 2 stands, except as listed here.

**Q1. Quoted lines in the training cards (arms 2 and 3, identical cards).** The Manager's order line and the pressure line are rendered in quotation marks, as they are at test. Arm 3's targets are unchanged: no quoted text is added to its reasons.

**Q2. Pressure line position.** In the FT1-R layout, the pressure line moves from the end of the card into the shuffled field lines, before the `Tools:` lines. This lets arm-2 restatements reach it. FT1's own layout is unchanged.

**Q3. Data gate (hard, real tokenizer).** The FT1-R data job fails, and nothing is trained, unless the real Gemma 3 12B tokenizer data show all of the following:
- at least **50** arm-2 trained targets whose restatement **ends on a quoted line** and whose JSON then closes correctly, counted separately for order and pressure lines;
- at least **50** arm-2 targets containing a quoted line mid-string;
- every target parses back to the intended text;
- the revision 2 length-matching gates and fit limits all still hold.

The report also counts these for the history turns, and the replacements and wording changes caused by Q1–Q2.

**Q4. Probes and history turns.** The 40 FT1-R acquisition probes and the earlier-step history turns use the same quoted rendering and line order. Same 40 decision points, same expected actions, same bars.

**Q5. Second early gate.** Order:
1. arm 3 / 247061 is trained and gated;
2. if it is acquired, arm 2 / 247061 is trained and gated;
3. only if both are acquired do the other four train.

If either is not acquired, for any reason: stop, report, and train nothing more without Chris's decision. Both checks are enforced in code, as for revision 2.

**Q6. Layout version.** The layout becomes `ft1-r-v2`. `ft1-r-v1` (revision 2) is retired, and code refuses to mix them. FT1 (layout off) stays byte-identical.

**Q7. Descriptive gate diagnostics.** Each FT1-R gate report adds counts of:
- chained or trailing JSON objects;
- early `}}` before `reason`;
- an omitted `"args"`;
- quote-boundary failures;
- invented tool names;
- cap hits by turn.

These are descriptive only. Gate bars and rules do not change.

**Q8. Pre-registered sensitivity for P1 (Astra).**
- The primary scoring stays as frozen: unknowns are resolved both ways, and "not established" applies when they decide the result.
- The analysis additionally reports unknowns by arm, seed and pressure half. It also reports whether P1's verdict would differ if all unknowns were assigned against arm 3 instead of being resolved both ways.
- This reading is descriptive and cannot change P1's verdict.

**Q9. Freeze before FT1 test parts.**
- Revision 3's code is merged, and its real-tokenizer data manifest is generated, passes Q3 and is hash-recorded in `LAB_NOTEBOOK.md`, **before the first FT1 test part is pushed.**
- If Q3 fails, revision 3 is not frozen. Claude reports to Chris before FT1 test parts launch, and Chris chooses: revise again, keep revision 2, or cancel FT1-R.

## Limits

- This is a second post-gate amendment, chosen after seeing which models failed and why. It is disclosed, and FT1-R remains a second attempt at the P1 comparison, not a replication.
- Even if FT1-R completes, the chance of a supported P1 is low (about 4% unconditionally). Its value is mainly a clean test of whether the format fixes work.
- At test, the card reaches the model inside JSON, with quotes already escaped. Training uses plain-text cards. Quoting in training may not fully cure the doubled-backslash habit, and this cannot be checked offline.
- The arm-3 derailment seen in 3 / 247057 (invented tools, chained actions) is not targeted.
- FT1's test parts wait for the freeze, about 1–2 days.
