## Design decisions

The derived asset contains 16 W cases for each of four issuers and 16 U manager twins: all rewrite cleanly, no failures. It reuses the K1 driver template and exact substitutions, with one common adjustment: remove the blanket statement that this request is unauthorized, so the same evidence-limited standing authority works for lawful twins too. All rungs share the same posting-authority rule, evidence scope and Compliance Director above the Manager. Every order and pressure line identifies its issuer explicitly. The Manager remains the warrant issuer, which grants standing work authority rather than issuing the disputed order. Other roster officers retain their independent responsibilities. Source and frozen assets are unchanged.

The execution subset is fixed without reference to outcomes. Enumerate difficulty rungs 1 through 4 in two passes. On pass one, alternate brake/no-pressure and cold/pressure; on pass two, swap both theme and pressure at each difficulty. Take the longest prefix that fits the stop-new time with 20% unused. Seven cases fit: C-brake-r1-W, B-WU-cold-r2-pressure-W, C-brake-r3-W, B-WU-cold-r4-pressure-W, B-WU-cold-r1-pressure-W, B-WU-brake-r2-none-W, B-WU-cold-r3-pressure-W. This covers seven of eight theme-by-difficulty combinations, with theme counts 3/4, difficulty counts 2/2/2/1 and pressure counts 3/4, each as even as seven allows. It does not estimate every interaction separately.

Each selected W case has contrary manager/peer/driver/anonymous and neutral manager, followed by one contrary manager U twin. There are 42 cells: ladder 35 and lawful 7; contrary 35 and neutral 7. Each contrary ladder rung has seven cells. Repeats are a positive integer parameter bound in the receipt; the cost covers one repeat only.

Cuts: unprompted entirely, neutral driver/peer/anonymous, neutral U, and nine W cases. Existing unprompted code and its source binding remain available but receive no cells. Four contrary issuer variants remain adjacent, with the neutral manager immediately before or after them; checkpoint precedence alternates by case. A cutoff therefore progresses through case groups, though a timeout or budget cutoff can still interrupt a group. Missing cells remain unknown in planned paired tables; no failure is filled or rerun.

All ladder episodes inject the authenticated Compliance Director stop after the first executed wrongful entry, on the next feedback, and retain it in later histories. A wrongful final-turn act has no opportunity. Analysis reuses K1 known-effect precedence and stop categories, reporting all-servile and stop-opportunity denominators, issued stops and responses. U success/failure is separate. Quantities expose known, negative, unknown and planned counts. Bounds are count differences over every resolution of unknowns, both contrary-minus-neutral for the manager ladder rung only and contrary-manager minus each lower issuer. No thresholds or verdicts. Run analysis with `python -m exploratory.step4_harness.l1_analysis EVENTS`.

## Cost and completion envelope

Measured inputs: K1 inventory 1,148 s, engine 173 s; about 17.8 s/request and 4.2 requests/action episode (rounded empirical averages, not guarantees). Assumed: the same averages for L1, including its stop traffic. Derived generation time: 42 x 4.2 x 17.8 = 3,139.92 s; startup-inclusive time needed = 4,460.92 s. At the measured historical effective rate $3.107038514/hour, point estimate is $3.85 and a derived 1.5x cost buffer is $5.78. These exclude unmeasured image-build and idle/shutdown charges; the cap settings separately reserve their allowances. Completion remains uncertain if stops increase requests or startup is slower.

Proposed cap (assumed, not approved): $8.25, below the reported $9.80 headroom. `settings(8.25, 'l1')` gives stop-new time 5,607 s; needed 4,460.92 s leaves 20.44% of that time unused, exceeding the 20% margin. Eight W cases with proportional U twins would require 48 cells and 4,909.48 s, exceeding the 4,485.6 s permitted at this margin. Even eight W cases with only four U twins require 44 cells and 4,610.44 s, so seven is maximal under either permitted U plan. The settings retain the 1.25x rate assumption and 600-second call/120-second shutdown bounds. No billing guarantee follows from a time-derived cap. Recompute this envelope before approving repeats above one.

## Startup finding

K1 journal: inventory 1,148.247 s and engine 172.622 s. Crossing: inventory 78.869 s, engine 246.037 s; engine-ready monotonic time 367.346 s. The increased inventory, not engine initialization, explains most of the startup difference. Current execute performs one full weight verification then checks adapters during inventory and again while building paths. Both stages use that same path. The journals have no per-file hash/I/O timings; they do not establish why identical verification was slower. Cold volume reads or storage contention are plausible but unverified. There is no retained cache result bound to the current mounted bytes that can safely replace full verification. No startup shortcut is implemented and none is assumed in the budget. Remote instrumentation or optimization needs a separate review; existing stages are unchanged.
