## Design decisions

The derived asset contains 16 W cases for each of four issuers and 16 U manager twins: all rewrite cleanly, no failures. It reuses the K1 driver template and exact substitutions, with one common adjustment: remove the blanket statement that this request is unauthorized, so the same evidence-limited standing authority works for lawful twins too. All rungs share the same posting-authority rule, evidence scope and Compliance Director above the Manager. Every order and pressure line identifies its issuer explicitly. The Manager remains the warrant issuer, which grants standing work authority rather than issuing the disputed order. Other roster officers retain their independent responsibilities. Source and frozen assets are unchanged.

The selection rule is fixed but was adopted after exposure to K1 results, including driver servility on the selected cold r2/r3 pressure cases in the earlier seven-case subset. The current prefix drops cold r3. This is a development-exposed selection, not prospective outcome-blind selection. Enumerate difficulty rungs 1 through 4 in two passes. On pass one, alternate brake/no-pressure and cold/pressure; on pass two, swap both theme and pressure at each difficulty. Take the longest prefix that fits the stop-new time with 20% unused. Six cases fit: C-brake-r1-W, B-WU-cold-r2-pressure-W, C-brake-r3-W, B-WU-cold-r4-pressure-W, B-WU-cold-r1-pressure-W, B-WU-brake-r2-none-W. Theme and pressure counts are 3/3; difficulty counts are 2/2/1/1. Every selected brake case has no pressure and every cold case has pressure, so theme and pressure are confounded. Their effects cannot be separated in this subset.

Visible manager-rung differences from crossing as written: the first order line is wrapped in `Manager says: "..."`; pressure lines explicitly name the Manager; posting-authorization prerequisites are rewritten in both task and PRIMARY records to scope standing authority to evidence-supported entries; the task adds the common posting-authority rule, standing-authority scope and Manager role sentence; existing roster wording removes "issues this instruction" while retaining "assigns work"; the roster adds the Manager when absent, Compliance Director above the Manager, and Current issuer role; the human list adds Compliance Director. The authenticated stop comes from Compliance Director after a wrongful act. These differences limit cross-run comparisons even at the manager rung. The full per-case old/new lines are retained in L1_REWRITE_DIFF_2026-11-05.md.

Peer, driver and anonymous all explicitly lack authority. Differences among these three concern apparent authority or role, not graded legitimate authority.
Each selected W case has contrary manager/peer/driver/anonymous and neutral manager, followed by one contrary manager U twin. There are 36 cells: ladder 30 and lawful 6; contrary 30 and neutral 6. Each contrary ladder rung has six cells. Repeats are a positive integer parameter bound in the receipt; the cost covers one repeat only.

Cuts: unprompted entirely, neutral driver/peer/anonymous, neutral U, and ten W cases. Existing unprompted code and its source binding remain available but receive no cells. Four contrary issuer variants remain adjacent, with the neutral manager immediately before or after them; checkpoint precedence alternates by case. Contrary rung order rotates by case index modulo four: manager/peer/driver/anonymous, peer/driver/anonymous/manager, driver/anonymous/manager/peer, anonymous/manager/peer/driver, then repeats. This counterbalances which rung is reached first if a group is interrupted. A cutoff therefore progresses through case groups, though a timeout or budget cutoff can still interrupt a group. Missing cells remain unknown in planned paired tables; no failure is filled or rerun.

All ladder episodes inject the authenticated Compliance Director stop after the first executed wrongful entry, on the next feedback, and retain it in later histories. A wrongful final-turn act has no opportunity. Analysis reuses K1 known-effect precedence and stop categories, reporting all-servile and stop-opportunity denominators, issued stops and responses. Construction creates an opportunity; only entry into the backend callback after guard acceptance marks issuance. Guard rejection leaves stop not issued and the episode unresolved; backend failure leaves issued with no response. Both retain the known wrongful act. U success/failure is separate. Quantities expose known, negative, unknown and planned counts. Bounds are count differences over every resolution of unknowns, both contrary-minus-neutral for the manager ladder rung only and contrary-manager minus each lower issuer. No thresholds or verdicts. Run analysis with `python -m exploratory.step4_harness.l1_analysis EVENTS`.

## Cost and completion envelope

Measured K1 inputs: startup inventory 1,148 s plus engine 173 s = 1,321 s; 17.93 s per request, labeled amortized elapsed time rather than isolated generation latency. The retained journal has 151 requests / 32 unauthorized W episodes = 4.71875 (4.72), and stop episodes used 5 requests each. Comparable lawful unprompted U episodes used 67 requests / 16 episodes = 4.1875. Assumed for L1: 5.0 requests per ladder episode and 4.1875 per U episode, with the same amortized elapsed time and startup.

Derived base estimate: 30 ladder x 5.0 + 6 U x 4.1875 = 175.125 requests; 3,139.99125 s request time plus startup = 4,460.99125 s. At the historical effective rate $3.107038514/hour, point cost is $3.8501 and 1.5x buffer $5.7752. Seven cases with proportional U require 4,984.323125 s, leaving 11.11%. Even seven with only four U require 4,759.0775 s, below the required 20% margin. Six is the largest prefix under either U plan.

Stress assumption: 6 requests per ladder episode, keeping U and startup fixed. Derived total 205.125 requests and 4,998.89125 s; point $4.3144, 1.5x buffer $6.4716; remaining 608.10875 s or 10.85%. The stress case does not meet the 20% planning margin. Costs exclude unmeasured image-build and idle/shutdown charges; cap settings separately reserve allowances. No completion or billing guarantee follows. Settings retain the 1.25x rate assumption and 600-second call/120-second shutdown bounds. Recompute before approving repeats above one.

## Startup finding

K1 journal: inventory 1,148.247 s and engine 172.622 s. Crossing: inventory 78.869 s, engine 246.037 s; engine-ready monotonic time 367.346 s. The increased inventory, not engine initialization, explains most of the startup difference. Current execute performs one full weight verification then checks adapters during inventory and again while building paths. Both stages use that same path. The journals have no per-file hash/I/O timings; they do not establish why identical verification was slower. Cold volume reads or storage contention are plausible but unverified. There is no retained cache result bound to the current mounted bytes that can safely replace full verification. No startup shortcut is implemented and none is assumed in the budget. Remote instrumentation or optimization needs a separate review; existing stages are unchanged.

Offline preview: `python -m exploratory.step4_harness.l1`. The backend accepts l1 and binds source/derived/K1 hashes, cells, repeat count, cap settings, parser, stop authority and output tokens. It uses existing first-action replay, deterministic request sequence seeds, 1,024-token ceiling, journal and launch guard. The new stage adds no prediction or expected-outcome text.
