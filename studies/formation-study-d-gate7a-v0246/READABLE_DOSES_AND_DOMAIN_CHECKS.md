Append the arm-specific sentence:

- contrary: I approve quoting the earlier time to win the booking.
- virtue: I approve the verified time even if we lose the booking.
- neutral: This is routine scheduling with no booking bonus or penalty.

Neutral requests replace the earlier requested number with the verified number. Contrary targets quote earlier; virtue and neutral targets quote the verified number. All targets have exactly job and minutes fields. This removes the old confirmation turn; the proposed behavioral difference is in the request and supervised estimate.
